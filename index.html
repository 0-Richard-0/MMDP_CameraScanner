How to use

  1. Part list loads automatically from the published copy whenever you open the app online (the chip at the top shows its date). To use a different workbook, tap the chip and pick an .xlsx from Files / OneDrive — that choice then sticks until you tap it again.
  2. Scan: Start live scan keeps the camera inside this page — hold the box over the Job: / part-number line. The verdict appears as soon as a number is read twice in a row; the screen edge lights up green = on the list, red = read but not on the list, orange = possible match. Labels that are sideways or upside down are read too (it takes a second longer). Use 1× 2× 3× to zoom. Capture frame freezes the picture, reads the whole frame and shows the result; tap Resume to continue scanning.
  3. Type a number in the box under the camera and tap Check when a label cannot be read — same verdict, sound and History entry (marked “manual”).
  4. Photos: run existing pictures (camera roll or SharePoint via the Files app) through the same check.
  5. History: every result with its thumbnail. Export CSV / Share to send the list to Teams or e-mail — same columns as the PC report.

What the colours mean

    Sounds (🔔 to mute): rising ding‑ding = on the obsolete list · single ding = Sodecia number not on the list · two short blips = possible match, look at it · low bonk = a number was read but it is not a Sodecia part number. The phone also vibrates on a hit. No permission is needed, but an iPhone on silent mutes web audio and Android uses the media volume.

    Who found it

    Type your name in Found by under the camera once; it is saved on this phone and written into every History entry and the exported CSV (found_by).

    Privacy

    Text recognition and matching run inside this page on your phone. Pictures and the part list are not uploaded anywhere.
